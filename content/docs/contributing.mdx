#  Contributing to Ply

Everyone is welcome to contribute — whether you're fixing a typo, sharing an idea, or building a new feature.

We believe in building Ply **together**, through small steps, open discussions, and shared curiosity.

---

##  How You Can Help

- 🐞 **Found a bug?** Open an issue and tell us what went wrong  
- 📚 **Docs unclear?** Help us improve the explanation  
- ✨ **Have an idea?** Share it in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Dziqha/ply/discussions)  
- 💻 **Want to code?** Pick any open issue and start small  

No contribution is too small — everything matters.

---

##  Collaborate with Us

We value friendly feedback, questions, and ideas. You don’t need permission to jump in.

Not sure how to start? Just ask in a discussion or issue — we’re here for you!

> Ply is built not just with code, but with people.

---

## Thank You

💙 Thanks for being here, seriously.

Your presence and willingness to contribute help shape Ply into something better — for everyone.

Let’s build it together.


> If you like this project, consider giving it a [⭐](https://github.com/Dziqha/ply) on GitHub, Your support helps more people discover the project and keeps us motivated to build more.
